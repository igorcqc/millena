Version: 1.0

# Purpose

This skill defines how to design landing pages for local businesses: clinics, stores, service providers, and other regionally-bound businesses.

Local business pages must build fast, tangible trust and make contact frictionless.

---

# Scope

Applies to businesses serving a specific geographic area: local services, clinics, retail stores, contractors, and similar.

# Local Visitor Psychology

Local visitors ask, in addition to the standard questions:

- "Do they serve my area?"
- "Are they legitimate and nearby?"
- "Can I reach them easily?"
- "What do people in my area say about them?"

---

# Priority Trust Signals

## Location Confirmation

State the service area or address clearly, ideally above the fold or very early on the page.

---

## Local Social Proof

Prioritize reviews and testimonials from recognizable local platforms (Google reviews especially) over generic testimonials — local visitors trust local validation.

---

## Real Presence Signals

- Real photos of the physical location, team, or work performed
- Years serving the community
- Recognizable local landmarks or service area descriptions

---

# Contact and Conversion Paths

Local business visitors often prefer direct, low-friction contact over forms:

- WhatsApp click-to-chat as a primary or parallel CTA (very effective in markets where WhatsApp is standard for business communication)
- Click-to-call phone number
- Simple form as an alternative, not the only path

See 05-cta.md and 07-forms.md — for local business, offering multiple contact channels typically outperforms a single form.

---

# Structure Adjustments

Relative to the default architecture (02), local business pages typically benefit from:

- Location/service area confirmation near the hero
- A "how it works" or "what to expect" section reducing uncertainty about the in-person or service experience
- Map or service area visual when relevant
- Local reviews prominently featured, not buried

---

# Copy Tone

- Warm, approachable, community-oriented tone (adjust for business type — a clinic differs from a contractor)
- Speak to the specific local context when relevant (neighborhood names, regional considerations)
- Avoid overly corporate or generic language that feels disconnected from a local, personal business

---

# Common Business Types and Adjustments

## Clinics / Healthcare

Emphasize credentials, cleanliness/safety, and patient testimonials. Booking/scheduling should be frictionless.

---

## Home Services / Contractors

Emphasize before/after visuals, licensing/insurance, and fast quote turnaround.

---

## Retail Stores

Emphasize product visuals, in-store experience, and directions/hours.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Hiding location/service area information
- Relying only on a form when WhatsApp/phone would convert better for the audience
- Using generic corporate stock photography instead of real local photos
- Ignoring local reviews in favor of generic testimonials
- Overly formal tone mismatched with a personal, local business

---

# Implementation Rules

The AI must:

1. Confirm service area/location early in the page.
2. Prioritize local social proof (Google reviews especially).
3. Offer WhatsApp/phone alongside or instead of a form-only path, when appropriate to the market.
4. Use real photos of the location, team, or work when available.
5. Match tone to the specific local business type.

---

# Quality Checklist

□ Location/service area confirmed early

□ Local social proof prioritized

□ Multiple contact paths offered (form + WhatsApp/phone)

□ Real, local photography used or clearly flagged as needed

□ Tone matches the business type and community

---

# AI Instructions

Always ask what geographic area the business serves and what contact method their customers actually prefer before defaulting to a form-only structure.
