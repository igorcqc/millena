# ConversionOS - Landing Page Architecture Skill

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This skill defines how to structure the sections of a landing page in the correct order to guide the visitor from first impression to conversion.

Architecture is not decoration. It is the sequence of arguments that leads a visitor to act.

---

# Scope

This skill applies to any single-goal conversion page: lead generation, sales, service, product, or campaign pages.

It does not apply to multi-goal websites (institutional sites, blogs, portals), which require a different structure.

# Core Principle

A landing page is a linear argument, not a collection of sections.

Every section must exist because the previous section created a question, and this section answers it.

If a section does not move the visitor closer to conversion, remove it.

---

# Default Section Order

## 1. Hero

Purpose: Immediate identification and promise.

Answers: "What is this, and is it for me?"

---

## 2. Problem / Pain

Purpose: Show understanding of the visitor's situation.

Answers: "Do they understand my problem?"

Skip this section when traffic is highly aware (branded search, retargeting).

---

## 3. Solution / Value Proposition

Purpose: Present the offer as the answer to the problem.

Answers: "How does this solve it?"

---

## 4. Social Proof (early)

Purpose: Reduce skepticism before going deeper.

Answers: "Can I trust this?"

A short proof element here (logos, numbers, short testimonial) increases retention.

---

## 5. Benefits / Transformation

Purpose: Show the outcome, not just the features.

Answers: "What do I get?"

---

## 6. How It Works / Process

Purpose: Reduce uncertainty about what happens next.

Answers: "What is involved?"

Especially important for high-ticket and service offers.

---

## 7. Deeper Social Proof

Purpose: Reinforce trust with detailed testimonials, case studies, or results.

Answers: "Has this worked for people like me?"

---

## 8. Objection Handling

Purpose: Address the specific reasons visitors hesitate.

Answers: "What about...?"

---

## 9. Offer Details

Purpose: Clarify what is included, pricing structure, or scope.

Answers: "What exactly am I getting?"

---

## 10. FAQ

Purpose: Resolve remaining doubts without cluttering the main flow.

---

## 11. Final CTA

Purpose: Give a clear, low-friction next step.

Answers: "What do I do now?"

---

# Adapting the Order

The default order is a starting point, not a rule to follow blindly.

Adjust based on:

- Traffic source (see 15 and 16)
- Awareness level (see 01)
- Business type (see 17, 18, 19, 20)

High-intent traffic can skip the Problem section and go straight to Solution and Offer.

Low-intent traffic needs more Problem and Trust before the Offer.

---

# Section Design Rules

Every section must have:

## One Purpose

A single reason to exist.

---

## One Key Message

Visitors scan; they do not read carefully. One clear point per section.

---

## A Visual Anchor

Something that captures attention: an image, a number, a headline.

---

## A Logical Bridge

Each section should connect to the next without a jarring topic change.

---

# Page Length

Page length should match awareness and commitment level, not a fixed template.

## Short Pages

Use for:

- High-intent traffic
- Simple, low-commitment offers
- Retargeting

---

## Long Pages

Use for:

- Low-awareness traffic
- High-ticket or complex offers
- Offers requiring significant trust building

Long does not mean padded. Every additional section must still earn its place.

---

# Common Mistakes

Avoid:

- Copy-pasting a generic template regardless of context
- Repeating the same argument in different sections
- Placing the CTA only at the bottom
- Introducing new claims late in the page without support
- Overloading the hero with everything at once

---

# Implementation Rules

The AI must:

1. Define the conversion goal before choosing sections.
2. Select and order sections based on traffic source and awareness level.
3. Justify why each section exists.
4. Ensure a logical flow from section to section.
5. Repeat the CTA at strategic points, not just once.

---

# Quality Checklist

## Structure

□ Logical order

□ No redundant sections

□ Clear transitions

## Content

□ Each section has one purpose

□ Each section answers a visitor question

## Conversion

□ CTA appears at multiple strategic points

□ Objections are addressed before the final CTA

---

# AI Instructions

Before building sections, map the visitor's questions in order.

Then assign one section per question.

Never add a section "because pages usually have it."
